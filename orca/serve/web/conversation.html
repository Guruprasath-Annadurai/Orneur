Before you start

Understand what temporary mode is intended to do before using it. These are proposed behaviors, not verified privacy guarantees.

Conversation history
Proposed: temporary chats do not appear in normal history. This must be proven through storage and account-level testing.
Data retention
Not yet verified. Temporary does not guarantee zero retention, automatic erasure, anonymity, or zero logs.
Access and model readiness
Genesis, Novus and Aeternum are the only native families. Model eligibility and temporary-session access are not verified.

This preview creates no session. Privacy wording must match audited retention, memory, logging, and deletion behavior before release.

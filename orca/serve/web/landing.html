Questions

FAQ

How is Orca different from other AI assistants?
Orca retrieves from your actual sources before answering, then checks its own claims against what it retrieved. When it can't verify something, it says so instead of presenting it with the same confidence as a checked fact.
What happens when Orca isn't confident in an answer?
The response is labeled "Partially supported" or "Not checked" rather than "Grounded," and the specific unsupported claim is called out — never silently upgraded to look more certain than it is.
Is my data used to train your models?
No — production conversation data is not used to train Orca's models without separate, explicit opt-in.
What model tiers are actually available today vs. coming soon?
Genesis and Novus are trained, evaluated, and available now. Aeternum is still in development — it has no trained model yet, so we're not claiming capabilities for it until it does.
How do you handle sensitive or personal information in responses?
Personal information is flagged for your awareness, not silently rewritten or stripped — it's usually your own data and altering it silently would be worse. Credentials and secrets are the exception: those are actively redacted.
Is Orca SOC 2 compliant?
Not yet — SOC 2 is on our roadmap as we grow. See our full, current security posture on the Trust & Security page.
